                          COMPANY PROFILE

Health Systems
Integrated Healthcare Delivery Network
------------------------------------------------------------------------

OVERVIEW
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Health Systems is a regional integrated healthcare delivery network
headquartered in Raleigh, North Carolina. The organization operates
acute-care hospitals, outpatient clinics, and specialty imaging centers
across the southeastern United States, serving more than 1.4 million
patients annually.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
KEY FACTS
------------------------------------------------------------------------
Founded:                1987
Headquarters:           Raleigh, NC
Facilities:             6 hospitals, 34 outpatient clinics
Employees:              ~11,200
Annual Patient Visits:  1.4 million
Fiscal Year End:        June 30

------------------------------------------------------------------------
MISSION
------------------------------------------------------------------------
To deliver compassionate, coordinated, and technology-enabled care that
improves the health of the communities we serve.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
TECHNOLOGY ENVIRONMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Electronic Health Record (EHR) platform serving all facilities
- Enterprise imaging and PACS across 3 imaging centers
- Content services platform for clinical and administrative documents
- Cloud migration initiative underway for archival and DR workloads

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STRATEGIC PRIORITIES (FY2026)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Consolidate content repositories onto a single governed platform.
2. Expand digital imaging archive capacity by 40%.
3. Migrate disaster-recovery workloads to cloud infrastructure.
4. Improve clinician document-retrieval times by 25%.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRIMARY CONTACTS (ROLES)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Chief Information Officer
- Director of Procurement
- Enterprise Imaging Program Lead
