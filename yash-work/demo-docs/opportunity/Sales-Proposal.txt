========================================================================
EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
========================================================================
Health Systems is renewing and expanding its enterprise content and
imaging platform to support a three-year modernization roadmap. This
proposal outlines the recommended solution, scope, and commercial terms
to renew the existing platform and add capacity for the organization's
cloud migration and imaging-archive expansion initiatives.

========================================================================
UNDERSTANDING YOUR OBJECTIVES
========================================================================
1. Consolidate clinical and administrative content onto one governed
   platform.
2. Expand imaging-archive capacity by 40% over 18 months.
3. Reduce clinician document-retrieval time by 25%.
4. Migrate disaster-recovery workloads to cloud infrastructure.

========================================================================
RECOMMENDED SOLUTION
========================================================================
- Content Services Platform renewal (enterprise tier)
- Enterprise Imaging Module with expanded archive tier
- Cloud DR add-on for automated failover
- Premier Support (24x7) with named technical account manager

========================================================================
IMPLEMENTATION APPROACH
========================================================================
Phase 1 (Weeks 1-4):   Discovery, environment assessment, success plan
Phase 2 (Weeks 5-10):  Platform renewal, configuration, integrations
Phase 3 (Weeks 11-16): Imaging expansion, DR enablement, cutover
Phase 4 (Ongoing):     Adoption support and quarterly business reviews
